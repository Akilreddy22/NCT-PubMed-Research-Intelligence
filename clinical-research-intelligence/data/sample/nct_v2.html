A Phase 3 Study of Pembrolizumab Versus Standard Chemotherapy in Advanced Melanoma

Registry ID: NCT99000001

Brief Summary

This study compares pembrolizumab with standard chemotherapy in adults with unresectable or metastatic melanoma.

Detailed Description

Participants are randomly assigned to receive either pembrolizumab every 3 weeks or investigator-choice chemotherapy. Tumour response is assessed every 9 weeks.
